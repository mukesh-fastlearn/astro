The Philosophy of Jyotish

“Astrology is not about surrendering to fate; it is the map that helps you navigate the ocean of Karma. Through awareness and right action (Pariharam), we can alter the course of our destiny.”

Vedic tradition holds that planetary placements at birth indicate karmic patterns, and that remedies — mantra, gemstone therapy, charitable acts — help mitigate hardship and amplify favourable periods. The chart shows the pattern; what you do with it remains yours.

How It Is Calculated

How every chart on this site is built, step by step.

Step One

Astronomical Positions

Your birth moment is converted to precise geocentric longitudes for all nine grahas using a professional ephemeris — the same astronomy used in observatories.

2

Step Two

Sidereal Conversion

Those tropical positions are shifted to the sidereal zodiac using the Lahiri ayanamsa, including drift and nutation, as Vedic astrology requires.

3

Step Three

Chart Construction
